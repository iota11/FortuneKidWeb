Roof Band

An instrument-simulation series. You climb to a rooftop, pick up an instrument, and play a two-minute song. No note charts scrolling at you, no failure state — the goal is the feeling of having played something.

Concept

Rhythm games ask you to read. Roof Band asks you to listen. Each instrument is simulated rather than charted — strings, pads, and keys respond to where and how you touch them, and the arrangement fills in around whatever you play. A first-time player sounds competent in about forty seconds, which is the entire design target.

It ships as a series rather than a single title: one rooftop, one band lineup, one small set of songs per release, so the follow-up can respond to what players actually replayed.

Cadence

Scoped to a build window a team our size can close, with the instrument simulation and audio engine carrying over between releases. The expensive part is built once; each release adds a lineup, a rooftop, and songs.

  • Original IP, no licensed music or characters.
  • In development for the App Store and Google Play.
  • Engine and audio layer shared with future titles.

Tie-in merch

Each band member exists as a game-ready model and a print-ready model at the same time. Once players have met a character in a release, that character can go to the printer as a made-to-order figure — no separate sculpt, no minimum run, no licensing partner in the middle.

The instruments print too, at desk scale. They are the same meshes the player touched.
