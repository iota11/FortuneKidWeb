About

Fortune Kid Inc. is a New Jersey–based interactive-entertainment company (software publisher) building a growing portfolio of original mobile games and physical collectibles tied to its own game IP. We ship small, high-craft titles on a fast cadence, and pair them with made-to-order merchandise players can hold.
